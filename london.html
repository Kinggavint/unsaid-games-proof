What we do in London

Our London team champions European publishing and community. We host playtests in neighborhood pubs, meet with partners across the UK and EU, and collaborate with local creators to keep our games approachable and unforgettable.

  • Publishing and platform partnerships across the UK and Europe
  • Community events and weekly playtests
  • Student outreach, talks, and internships with local programs

We’re typically based around Shoreditch and the South Bank, but we love to travel across the city for events. If you’re a studio, streamer, or venue looking to collaborate, we’d love to hear from you.
