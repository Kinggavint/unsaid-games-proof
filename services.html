Frequently asked questions

How do you price projects? +

We offer fixed-price scopes for well-defined deliverables and time-and-materials for exploratory work.

  • Game Design: typically packaged (e.g., design doc, systems pass, levels) or weekly retainers.
  • Consulting: hourly or weekly, with a written report and roadmap options.
  • Prototyping: milestone-based fixed bids or sprint-based retainers.

After a short call, we’ll provide an estimate with scope, milestones, and options.

What are typical timelines? +
  • Game Design: 2–6 weeks for a design doc and core systems; ongoing support available.
  • Consulting: 1–2 weeks for an audit and recommendations; monthly advisory available.
  • Prototyping: 2–8 weeks depending on complexity, platform, and playtest cadence.

Timelines depend on scope clarity, feedback speed, and technical complexity. We align on milestones up front.

Can I see your project portfolio? +

Yes. Check out Our Games for public work. We can also share private case studies and prototypes under NDA upon request.

Need something specific? Tell us your genre, platform, or mechanic and we’ll send relevant examples.
