Project timelines — what to expect

How long does a typical project take? +

Timelines vary by scope. As a baseline:

  • Game Development: 6–12 weeks for a polished prototype; 3–6+ months for full production.
  • Consulting: 1–2 weeks for an audit and report; ongoing advisory available monthly.
  • Art Asset Creation: 1–4 weeks depending on volume and style exploration.
What factors affect the schedule? +

Scope clarity, feedback turnaround time, technical complexity (e.g., netcode), and platform requirements are the biggest drivers. We’ll align on milestones and dependencies up front.

Do you support rush/expedited timelines? +

Yes. We offer expedited options when capacity allows. Rush work may include a premium to cover after-hours and parallel staffing. Tell us your deadline and we’ll propose a realistic plan.
