What we do in Tokyo

Our Tokyo studio explores fast, joyful play on mobile and console. We prototype new mechanics, refine tactile interfaces, and draw inspiration from arcades, cafes, and the city’s unmatched rhythm.

  • Rapid prototyping and R&D on new mechanics
  • Mobile-first UX and localization for APAC
  • Artist and creator collaborations across the city

You’ll find us meeting around Akihabara and Shibuya, sharing builds and gathering feedback from passionate local players.
